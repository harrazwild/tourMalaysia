Cameron Highland


Cameron Highlands, Malaysia's premier hill station, is a refreshing retreat known for its cool climate, lush tea plantations, and scenic landscapes. Located in Pahang, this highland paradise offers breathtaking views, vibrant strawberry farms, and colorful flower gardens. Visitors can explore the iconic Boh Tea Plantation, hike through mossy forests, and experience the charm of local markets. Whether you seek adventure, relaxation, or a taste of freshly brewed tea, Cameron Highlands promises an unforgettable escape into nature.
