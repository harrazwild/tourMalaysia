Melaka


Melaka, a UNESCO World Heritage City, is a historical gem where Malaysia’s rich past comes to life. Once a thriving trading port, the city is now famous for its well-preserved colonial architecture, vibrant Jonker Street Night Market, and iconic landmarks like A Famosa and Stadthuys. Visitors can explore the Melaka River, enjoy Peranakan cuisine, and immerse themselves in the city's unique blend of Malay, Chinese, and European influences. With its charming atmosphere and deep cultural heritage, Melaka offers a captivating journey through time.
